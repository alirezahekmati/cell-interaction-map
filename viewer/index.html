About

Signed, directed interactions merged from SIGNOR (CC BY 4.0) and OmniPath (an aggregator; see the licences of its resources). Compartments come from the Human Protein Atlas, UniProt and Human-GEM, plus some manual overrides. Positions are schematic, not physical, and a line means "reported", not "verified". Not peer reviewed.
