About

Signed, directed interactions merged from SIGNOR (CC BY 4.0) and OmniPath (an aggregator; see the licences of its resources). Compartments come from the Human Protein Atlas, UniProt and Human-GEM, plus some manual overrides. Positions are schematic, not physical, and a line means "reported", not "verified". Protein names, function and disease text: UniProt (CC BY 4.0), for information only, not medical advice. Drugs, clinical stages, disease associations, tractability, safety events, DepMap essentiality and tool compounds: Open Targets Platform (CC0; please cite Open Targets Platform, 2025). Druggability levels: Pharos/TCRD (NCATS). Not peer reviewed.
